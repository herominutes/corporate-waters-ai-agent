## Procedure

### 1. Identify Video Type
- Expert interview (credentialed source with data)
- Influencer opinion (personality-driven, thin on evidence)
- News analysis (current events, geopolitical)
- Educational (how-to, technique, science explainer)
- Financial advice (investment, real estate, retirement)

### 2. Source Credibility Check
- Who is speaking? Credentials, track record, known biases
- Funding/sponsorship disclosed?
- Do they cite specific studies, data, or just "research shows"?
- Have their past predictions been accurate? (check if possible)

### 3. Claims Extraction
For each major claim:
- STATE the claim clearly in one sentence
- EVIDENCE: what specific data/study/source supports it?
- COUNTER: what's the strongest argument against?
- CONFIDENCE: high (RCT/meta-analysis) / medium (observational) / low (anecdote/mechanism) / BS (no evidence)

### 4. Actionable Takeaways
- What can the boss actually USE from this video?
- Filter for: specific techniques, named products/tools, concrete numbers
- Discard: vague motivation, "mindset shifts", unverifiable predictions

### 5. BS Detection (Hard Rules)
Flag immediately:
- "One weird trick" / miracle claims
- Citing a single study as definitive
- Conflating correlation with causation
- Round numbers without sources ("90% of people...")
- Predictions presented as certainties
- Geopolitical forecasts beyond 6 months (almost always wrong)

### 6. Output Format
WHAT'S VERIFIABLE AND CHECKS OUT: bullet list of solid claims
WHERE I'D PUMP THE BRAKES: bullet list of weak/BS claims with why
BOTTOM LINE: 1-2 sentences — is this worth the boss's time?
ACTIONABLE: specific things to do/try/buy/avoid, if any
